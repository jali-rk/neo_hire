01 / Executive Mandate

Creative Autonomy Backed by Commercial Rigour.

Central Design & Technical Studio • Statutory Corporate Governance

Aphrodite (Pvt) Ltd is an emerging Sri Lankan fashion conglomerate structured on the European multi-house model. Rather than operating as a single standalone label, our central technical studio powers distinct apparel divisions spanning high-rotation island-wide retail distribution to stealth-mode luxury craftsmanship.

By anchoring new creative labels upon active wholesale revenue channels and tightly audited partner factories, we eliminate traditional startup volatility—offering Sri Lanka’s premier designers, pattern engineers, and master tailors long-term corporate stability alongside world-class R&D freedom.
